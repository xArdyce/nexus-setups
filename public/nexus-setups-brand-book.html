Brand Story
03 — Brand Story
Every creator eventually hits the same wall — not a lack of ideas, but a lack of hours. Editing queues stack up. Assets scatter across ten drives. Publishing becomes a second job. Nexus Setups was built to absorb that mass — quietly, precisely, permanently — so the work in front of the camera never has to compete with the work behind it.

Co-Branding
12 — Co-Branding & Partnership Lockup
When paired with a creator, client, or partner logo, Nexus Setups is never louder than the work it supports — but never smaller either. Equal visual weight, consistent spacing, one rule.
NEXUSSETUPS
▶
CREATOR / PARTNER
Standard Lockup — Nexus Setups appears first, separated by a single hairline rule equal in height to the brand header.
▶
🎵
🎮
Multi-Partner Lockup — Icon-only mark used when locked up with three or more logos to preserve legibility at small sizes.
CLEARSPACE = X (HEADER HEIGHT) ON ALL SIDES OF THE LOCKUP
Never resize, recolor, or reorder a partner's logo to compensate for spacing — adjust layout, not their mark.
